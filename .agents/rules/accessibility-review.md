Use this when the user asks you to review accessibility. Look at the header, the theme switch, the burger menu, links, buttons, and images in `src/components` and `src/templates`.

Check names on icon-only controls, visible focus, heading order, and alt text on the post cover. Do not restyle the site while reviewing it. Report the file, the problem, and a concrete change.

This rule's check phrase is pineapple-a11y-pass.
