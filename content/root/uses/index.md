## 🛠 What I use

Here, you can check out the equipment and tools I use on a day-to-day basis in details.

- Keyboard and mouse:
  - Logitech POP Keys Mechanical Wireless Keyboard
  - Logitech MX Master 2S
- Display and machine:
  - Dell UltraSharp U2720Q 27 Inch 4K UHD
  - PowerCube 4 Outlet - Socket multiplier
  - I use Macbook Pro 2021 16 inch M1 Max with 64 GB RAM and 1 TB SSD
  - Before, I used Macbook Pro 2015 15 inch with 16 GB RAM and 256 GB SSD
- Software:
  - I spent years in [Vim](https://www.vim.org/). I learned it at the beginning of my professional career, and a lot of this blog is about it. These days I write in [Cursor](https://cursor.com/) and [Claude Code](https://claude.com/product/claude-code).
  - I'm a Mac user, so [iTerm2](https://iterm2.com/) does the job perfectly.
  - [zsh](https://www.zsh.org/)
  - [oh-my-zsh](https://ohmyz.sh/)
  - [Arc](https://arc.net/) has been good to me, but it's sunsetting. I'm still figuring out what to use next.
  - Sometimes, you have to test some APIs or make WebSocket connections. Right about then, I roll out [Postman](https://www.postman.com/) to do it.
  - To let people schedule a call with me, I use [Calendly](https://calendly.com/nikolalsvk).
  - To manage my newsletter - [ConvertKit](https://partners.convertkit.com/oqs53bno6l4l) is awesome. That one is an affiliate link.

Thanks for reading. If you feel something is missing or you'd like to know more, [send me an email](mailto:nikola@pragmaticpineapple.com) or [ping me on Twitter](https://twitter.com/nikolalsvk), and I will add it.
